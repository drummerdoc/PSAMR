# Exec/CO2_ADV2D — smooth diagonal two-phase advection (order of accuracy)

A smooth periodic volume-fraction field $\alpha_1(x,y) = \bar\alpha + a\,\sin(2\pi k_x x)\sin(2\pi k_y y)$ ($\bar\alpha = 0.5$, $a = 0.25$, $k = 1$; `prob.alpha_pow = 3` gives the steeper $\sin^3\sin^3$ profile) is advected diagonally at uniform velocity $(u_0, v_0) = (100, 100)$ m/s and uniform pressure $P_0 = 80$ bar, phase 1 on the liquid branch at 270 K and phase 2 on the vapour branch at 330 K. With no pressure gradient there is no acoustic wave and the exact solution is the translated initial condition, so the $L_1(\alpha_1)$ error at `stop_time` against it gives a clean convergence rate for the non-conservative slot, and the partial mass $\alpha_1\rho_1$ ($\rho_1 \ne \rho_2$) gives a conserved-field companion. Relaxation, mass transfer and flash are off (pinned to 0) so the test is pure hydro. Periodic in both directions.

    make -j8 COMP=gnu DIM=2 USE_MPI=TRUE Eos_Model=PR
    for N in 32 64 128; do mpiexec -np 4 ./PSAMR2d.gnu.MPI.PS.PR.ex inputs amr.n_cell="$N $N" amr.plot_file=plt_$N; done

What to check (re-baselined 15 Nov 2026, after phase 2 was put on its own vapour branch at $P_0$, $T_2$ in `prob.H`; until then the phase-2 slot held the liquid state, the branch-locked EOS read it at 6.37 MPa against 8 MPa, and the mixture pressure varied with $\alpha_1$, so the earlier 6.80e-4 record was taken on an initial condition that launches acoustic waves). With the fix the initial pressure is uniform to 1.6e-12 relative and the velocity stays (100, 100) to 8e-11 m/s at `stop_time`. Measured with `PSAMR.ps_wp_limiter = none`, CFL 0.4, Mac llvm: at the deck defaults (amplitude 0.25, `ps_wp_transverse = 0`) $L_1(\alpha_1)$ = 1.09e-2 / 5.12e-3 / 1.42e-3 on 32² / 64² / 128², rates 1.09 and 1.85 (5.72e-3 at 64² with `ps_wp_order = 1`). Two things hold this below second order, each isolated by one run: without a transverse term the unsplit 2-D update lacks the $uv\,\Delta t\,\partial_{xy}$ cross term and is first order in $\Delta t$ (with `prob.v0 = 0` the same deck is second order to two decimals at amplitude 0.0025: 4.08e-6 / 1.02e-6 / 2.54e-7); and the contact-wave taper (DESIGN H-5) switches the correction off where the per-face $\alpha_1$ jump, $2\pi a/N$, exceeds $\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}} = 0.02$, which at amplitude 0.25 it does on 32² and 64². The configuration that measures the operator is `PSAMR.ps_wp_transverse = 1` with `prob.alpha_amp = 0.0025`: 8.05e-6 / 2.21e-6 / 6.49e-7, rates 1.87 and 1.77. The fine-box run's mass and $\rho E$ sums drift at round-off only. The scorer that differences the plotfile against the translated IC is `suite/adv2d_ooa.py` in the standalone repository (it assumes amplitude 0.25 and velocity (100, 100); pass the amplitude and velocity of any other run); the numbers are recorded in `docs/VERIFICATION.md` §7.2 and the full Stage 0 measurement in `docs/PLAN_high_order.pdf` §11.
