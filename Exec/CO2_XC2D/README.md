# Exec/CO2_XC2D — diagonal cross-critical Riemann problem (coarse-fine probe)

The two states of `CO2_B4` (liquid 270 K / 100 bar below, supercritical 350 K / 50 bar above) separated by the anti-diagonal $(x - x_{\mathrm{lo}})/L_x + (y - y_{\mathrm{lo}})/L_y = 2\,x_{\mathrm{diaph}}$, so the α contact crosses coarse-fine boundaries at 45° and the $y$-face fluctuation deposits for $\alpha_1$, $\mathcal{E}_1$, $\mathcal{E}_2$ — identically zero in the 1-D-in-$x$ B4 — are genuinely active. Its purpose is to make the coarse-fine treatment of the non-conservative slots measurable: 128² base with one refinement level on the pressure jump (`ptag`, 5 bar per cell), relaxation off, all faces interior-copy outflow, `PSAMR.sum_interval = 1` so the conserved sums print every step.

    make -j8 COMP=gnu DIM=2 USE_MPI=TRUE Eos_Model=PR
    mpiexec -np 4 ./PSAMR2d.gnu.MPI.PS.PR.ex inputs amr.max_level=1 max_step=40 amr.plot_int=40 amr.plot_file=plt_amr_
    mpiexec -np 4 ./PSAMR2d.gnu.MPI.PS.PR.ex inputs amr.max_level=0 amr.n_cell="256 256" stop_time=1.4334906e-4 amr.plot_int=100000 amr.plot_file=plt_ref256_

Configuration: wp with `PSAMR.ps_bl_reflux = 2` (on; the key is on/off and any nonzero value, the default 1 included, is the α co-move at the coarse-fine layer, without which $\rho_k = m_k/\alpha_k$ drifts when reflux corrects $m_k$ but not $\alpha_k$ and the fine ghosts inherit the broken decomposition) and `ps_wp_order = 2`. The protocol compares the two-level run with the uniform-256 reference at the same time along the $y = 0.5$ ray with `ray_diff.py plt_amr_00040 plt_ref256_00080` (fine cells against the uniform row, coarse cells against the mean of the uniform 2×2 block; zones: fine interior, coarse-fine edge, contact band, coarse elsewhere; then the cells where one run has removed a phase the other keeps). Measured 15 Nov 2026 with the symmetric co-move (`docs/VERIFICATION.md` §7.3 has the table and the old-rule column): fine interior $|\Delta\alpha_1|$ 4e-11, coarse-fine edge 1.8e-9 (1.2e-6 under the former one-sided rule), relative $|\Delta\mathcal{E}_1|$ 1.2e-4 at the edge, the contact band at coarse resolution 0.4. That ray diff is the regression target for any coarse-fine change, which must not grow the edge or interior numbers while keeping the 1-D B4 result bit-identical.

Status: without the α co-move (`PSAMR.ps_bl_reflux = 0`) the deck aborts at coarse step 22 (un-refluxed corridor deposits); with the default co-move it completes to `stop_time` (coarse step 56). The ray-diff numbers were measured under the retired split-flux path and need re-measuring under `wp` before being cited. Single-level, the scalar fields are transpose-symmetric to 1e-14 and the contact is one cell wide (10–90 %). See `docs/VERIFICATION.md`.
